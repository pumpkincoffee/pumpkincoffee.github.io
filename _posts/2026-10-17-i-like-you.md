I like you. I like your hat. I like your messy hair. I like the way you let one of your feet in front of mine. I like your flustered face. I like the way your eyes looked into mine. I like you, and I like you a lot.<br>
<br>
I want to walk with you, here in Tokyo. I want to bring you to Kasai Rinkai Koen in the dusk, sit on my favorite spot, watch the fish tournament, wait for Disney Land’s fireworks to appear, stare at the sky with planes going back and forth to Haneda, and share silly stories in between.<br>
<br>
I want to hike Olle trails with you. I want to take the ferry from Fukuoka to Jeju. I want to drive around Jeju with you. I want to show you Hamdeok beach. I want to visit Seogwipo with you. I want to sleep at the airbnb near the sea to show you the fishermen ahjussi who cheered me up during the lowest point of my life this year. I want to eat the huge mackerel and seaweed soup with you, I want to give you two-third of the portion. I want to eat jjukkumi in Yongsan with you. I want to show you the beautiful sunset in Hangang. I want to see all of those things one more time, with you.<br>
<br>
I want to go to Finland to see the aurora with you. I want to go to Istanbul and enjoy the city and its cats with you. I want to go to Mecca and Medina with you. I want to pray tahajjud and subuh next to you. And if God allows me to enter the heaven, I want to be there with you.
