You chose to sat far from my chair. You looked like your mind went blank when we caught each other’s eyes for the first time. I wish I could see myself, since I was as flustered as you were. You didn’t talk to me much, you talked with our friend the whole time. But I guess, I was just relieved that you didn’t. That way, I could see how your face lit up when you are excited about something, or when you laugh without holding back.<br>
<br>
It was brief, but I am glad that it happened. Our friend left us two in the room. And again, I was too shy to speak my mind out loud. Then you smiled when you said I must have been tired. I wish I could freeze the moment in my mind indefinitely.
