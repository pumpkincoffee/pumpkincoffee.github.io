"Lembah manah", that is the quality you asked me to always keep, to be someone who is full of humility, to be free of arrogance, to be kind-hearted and forgiving. Not that I should not be able to differentiate between being kind and being foolish, rather you asked me to hold two things at once. To be fierce in truth yet kind at heart.<br>
<br>
Granddaddy, it has been years since you appeared in my dream. I do not quite remember the last one. I think it was when I lost myself, when I was drowning too deeply in the ocean of darkness. I miss you. I want to see you when I am not drowning too. However, I guess you are doing very well up there. Did you see that Mom and I always visit your grave whenever I am home? Honestly, I am still wondering why Grandma never joined us. Perhaps it is too hard for her. What do I know? The only grief I have known well is losing you 11 years ago.<br>
<br>
Do you know that I hate my last memory with you, not the one where I read the Qur'an, I barely remember that one. It is this very silly thing when I took the TV remote control because I didn't like the show you watched. I don't even remember what the show was about, but I remember how I switched the channel. If I could turn back time, I would watch whatever show it was with you. I wouldn't change anything other than that.<br>
<br>
Granddaddy, whenever I fall, I always try to remember how I fell into the mud in the rice field, when you laughed so hard while the mud was all over my body. I always try to remember Soto bening that we ate at Mbah Min's. I always try to remember the sound of your Honda Astrea and how you always arrived at 3 in the morning from work. I always try to remember how you asked, while lying weak on the hospital bed, whether I was going to the US. I always try to remember the very few good childhood memories I have left in my seemingly forgetful brain.<br>
<br>
Granddaddy, thank you for being there when Dad was away working on another island. Thank you for being my second Dad. Mom always tells me the nicest things about you. I am very grateful that we shared several years together. That the mud memory becomes a reminder to always get back up whenever I fall terribly. That the ability to laugh during silly moments is a gift, because life is sometimes funny like that. That comfort food and sound are significant during difficult times. And that life is worth living.<br>
<br>
Granddaddy, I am not in the US. But you know what, I am able to communicate in English well. I have lived in Tokyo for almost five years. But yeah, I still can't communicate in Japanese well haha. I love it here. In fact, I think you might love it here as well.<br>
<br>
Granddaddy, I am doing well on my own now. Thank you for raising me to be this person. I still have a lot to work on though, like staying late to write you a letter while crying intermittently. I am also trying to do my best as the first to pursue higher education from our big family. I wish you were always there during every graduation I have. Because you were not, I skipped two haha. I wish you would visit my dreams before the next graduation.<br>
<br>
I wish you a magnificent resting place up there, I will ask God for that. Good night, Granddaddy, I will always love you.
